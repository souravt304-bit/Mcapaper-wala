Overview

Himachal Pradesh Technical University (HPTU) was established in 2010 with the objective to provide quality technical education in the state. The University is committed to create center of excellence in education, research and training in engineering, technology, management and pharmacy.

HPTU aims to develop professionals who are competent, responsible and globally aware. The university focuses on innovation, problem-solving and societal impact through its various programs.

About City

Hamirpur, located in the heart of Himachal Pradesh, is popularly known as the "Veer Bhumi" due to its rich tradition of valor and service. Nestled among the gentle Shivalik hills and scenic landscapes, the district enjoys a moderate climate throughout the year.

Hamirpur is rapidly emerging as a prominent educational hub, home to prestigious institutions such as NIT Hamirpur, IHM Hamirpur, and Himachal Pradesh Technical University (HPTU).
